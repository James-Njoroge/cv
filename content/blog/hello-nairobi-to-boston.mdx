Karibu — welcome to the first post.

I'm James. I grew up in Nairobi, Kenya, studied Computer Science and Applied
Mathematics at Colgate University, and finished my M.S. in Artificial
Intelligence at Boston University. This blog is where I'll think out loud about
the things I spend my days on: building machine-learning systems, wrangling
data, and — inevitably — football.

## Why AI, and why now

The tools have finally caught up to the ambition. A single engineer can now
take a messy real-world problem, model it, and ship something people actually
use — in weeks, not years. That collapse in the distance between _idea_ and
_working software_ is the most exciting thing I've seen in my career, and it's
where I want to spend it.

I care most about the unglamorous middle of that pipeline: the data collection,
the feature engineering, the evaluation loops that decide whether a model is
genuinely useful or just demo-ware.

## The football thread

If you look at [my projects](/projects), you'll notice a pattern. I built
**Footy Liveliness**, a system that predicts how exciting a Premier League
match will be _before kickoff_, and a separate NLP project forecasting team
performance across thirty seasons of EPL data.

Football is the perfect testbed for machine learning: rich historical data,
constant fresh outcomes to validate against, and a question everyone has an
opinion on. It keeps me honest — the model either picks the good games or it
doesn't.
