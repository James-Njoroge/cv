04 · IDENTITY
THE MARK
JN monogram in mono, a green status dot for “running,” and a progress bar at 66% — still training. Reads at 16px in a browser tab.
05 · MOTION & SCROLL
SCROLL = TRAININGPage progress drives one global clock: the header bar, the step counter, the loss readout, and the loss curve all read the same scalar 0→1.
EASINGcubic‑bezier(.2,.8,.2,1) at 700ms for reveals, 1100ms for bars. Nothing bounces.
REVEAL20px rise + fade, once per element, staggered 60ms in groups of three. Never re‑animates on scroll‑back.
TOKEN STREAM26ms per character, one pass, no loop. The cursor keeps blinking after it finishes.
06 · SPACE & VOICE
SPACE
4px base. Radii: 6 (tech tag), 12 (row), 14–18 (card), 99 (pill). Container 1180px, gutter 28px. Section rhythm: 120px bottom padding, 1px rule under every heading.
VOICE
The ML metaphor sits in the labels and headings; the sentences underneath are plain, specific, and human. One joke per section, maximum. Numbers only when they’re real.
